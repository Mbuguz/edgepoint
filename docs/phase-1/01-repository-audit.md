## Must be verified before publication

### Identity, contact, and local presence

- Exact registered name styling and company registration details, if they are to
  appear publicly.
- Primary telephone/WhatsApp numbers, monitored email addresses, business hours,
  and quotation recipient(s).
- Whether Edgepoint has a staffed, customer-facing physical location; its exact
  address and map coordinates if so.
- Actual service area and travel constraints. Kasarani targeting must describe a
  genuine service area, not imply an unverified office.
- Google Business Profile ownership, canonical name/address/phone data, and profile
  URL.
- Approved social profile URLs.

### Brand and evidence

- Logo variants, brand colors, fonts, icon policy, photography, and usage rights.
- Approved company overview, founding year, leadership/team biographies, and values.
- Verifiable projects, portfolio items, client permissions, metrics, testimonials,
  certifications, awards, memberships, vendors, and partnerships.
- Approved proof for response times, warranties, service-level commitments, and
  support availability.

### Service and product detail

- Exact deliverables, customer segments, coverage, exclusions, process, lead times,
  pricing policy, warranties, aftercare, and prerequisite site surveys for each
  service.
- Which expanded capabilities are currently delivered directly, partner-delivered,
  planned, or unavailable; named technical owners and evidence of competence.
- Starlink installer/reseller status and wording permitted by the brand owner.
- CCTV data-protection responsibilities, retention advice, and equipment warranty.
- Legal/technical authority for penetration testing, biometrics, electric fencing,
  alarms, telecommunications, fiber work, and any other regulated or high-risk work.
- Product catalogue, brands, stock status, specifications, prices, delivery,
  installation, returns, and warranty terms.
- Reseller/partner status and trademark permissions for Starlink, Google, Microsoft,
  MikroTik, Ubiquiti, TP-Link, Cisco, Huawei, Meta, and other named platforms.
- Whether online payment or ecommerce is required. It is not assumed in Phase 1.
- Industries actually served and whether industry-specific evidence exists.
- Whether sector systems (school, hospital, SACCO, HR/payroll, inventory, ERP) are
  existing Edgepoint products, third-party implementations, or custom-build
  capabilities, including ownership, security, support, and compliance boundaries.
- Existing support process, escalation contacts, and ticketing needs.

### Legal, privacy, and measurement

- Lawyer-approved Privacy Policy, Terms, Cookie Policy, acceptable-use/service

### Hosting and operations

- Recovery point/recovery time expectations and named release approvers.

## Publication rules

Until verified, do not:

- add an address, map pin, `LocalBusiness.address`, or opening hours;
- claim authorized reseller/partner/certified status;
- publish testimonials, customer logos, project metrics, “best/leading” claims,
  guaranteed uptime, response times, or ranking promises;
- create thin suburb pages solely to capture local queries;
- expose personal recipient addresses in markup or client-side scripts.

Unknown values should use explicit development placeholders that cannot enter a
production ZIP, enforced by a release checklist.
