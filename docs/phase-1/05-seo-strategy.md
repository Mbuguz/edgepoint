# Kasarani and Nairobi SEO Strategy

## Scope and evidence standard

This is a search-intent plan, not a ranking guarantee or a substitute for measured
keyword research. No Search Console, analytics, keyword-volume platform, competitor
set, Google Business Profile, or live-site crawl was available in Phase 1.

Before content production, validate terminology and demand with:

1. Google Search Console query/page data, if an existing property exists;
2. Google Keyword Planner and Trends scoped to Kenya/Nairobi;
3. manual mobile/desktop result review from the target market;
4. Google Business Profile categories/services and real customer enquiry language;
5. competitor content-gap review based on usefulness, not copy imitation.

Record query variants, intent, result features, approximate demand, difficulty,
priority, target URL, and evidence date in a keyword map. One primary page owns each
intent to prevent cannibalization.

## Geographic approach

- **Kasarani:** Primary service-area context on Home, Contact, Quote, and relevant
  service pages, provided the client confirms genuine coverage.
- **Nairobi County:** Main broader market; naturally explain availability, survey,
  installation, and support processes.
- **Greater Nairobi:** Secondary expansion language only where delivery is real.
- **Kenya:** Use for remote/deliverable services or actual national operations only.

Do not imply an office in Kasarani without a verified staffed location. Do not create
near-duplicate pages for Kasarani estates or Nairobi suburbs. A location page is
eligible only where there is distinct local evidence, logistics, projects, FAQs,
and meaningful content; otherwise strengthen canonical service pages.

### Industry search plan

Industry queries are secondary to service intent at launch. Candidate combinations
include “school ICT solutions Nairobi,” “IT support for SMEs Nairobi,” and “hotel
Wi-Fi solutions Nairobi,” but dedicated pages require unique operational content.

Prioritize industry research in this order:

1. markets with approved projects, expertise, and customer demand;
2. markets where several verified services form a coherent solution;
3. high-sensitivity sectors only after legal/security review.

Education, healthcare, finance, government, NGOs, hospitality, retail,
manufacturing, religious organizations, construction, real estate, SMEs, and
residential audiences can all appear as filters/pathways on the Industries index.
Until evidence exists, wording must say “solutions for” rather than “experience
serving,” “trusted by,” or equivalent proof claims.

## Content briefs and FAQs

Each indexable landing page should define:

- audience, job-to-be-done, primary/secondary intent, and funnel stage;
- unique proposition supported by evidence;
- H1 and logical H2/H3 outline;
- questions answered, process, prerequisites, limitations, service area;
- approved proof, image plan/rights, related entities, internal links, and CTA;
- author/reviewer expertise, sources, owner, and review date;
- unique title, description, canonical, robots, social image, and schema eligibility.

FAQ research comes from real sales/support questions, Search Console, result review,
and client interviews. Answers remain visible in page HTML and concise. FAQ schema
may be technically valid but is not expected to produce a Google rich result for
ordinary commercial sites; never add hidden or duplicated FAQ markup.

## Technical SEO specification

### Crawl, indexing, and canonicalization

- Production permits crawling; staging uses authentication plus `noindex` defense
  and is never linked publicly.
- Keep one HTTPS hostname. Redirect HTTP and alternate `www`/non-`www` host with
  one-hop 301s after canonical choice.
- Self-referencing canonical on indexable pages; canonical excludes campaign and
  non-content parameters.
- `noindex,follow` internal search and thin/duplicate archives; block or canonicalize
  faceted parameters based on tested behavior. Do not block a URL in robots.txt when
  its `noindex` must be seen.
- Custom 404 returns HTTP 404. Removed content returns 410 or the most relevant
  direct 301; never redirect all missing pages to Home.
- XML sitemap includes only canonical, indexable 200-status URLs with accurate
  modified dates. Submit through Search Console.
- `robots.txt` references sitemap and avoids blocking CSS/JS/images required for
  rendering. WordPress core/admin defaults are reviewed, not copied blindly.

### On-page and media

- Exactly one descriptive primary H1 is the default; nested headings follow content
  structure, not visual size.
- Unique title/description; descriptive links; semantic landmarks; breadcrumbs on
  interior pages.
- Images use meaningful filenames, dimensions, responsive sources, modern formats,
  and appropriate alt text. Lazy-load below-fold media, never the likely LCP image.
- Video requires transcript/captions and a lightweight poster/consent-aware embed.
- Open Graph and social metadata use approved assets; no unsupported claims.

### Structured data

Use JSON-LD generated from the same visible, verified source:

- `Organization` site-wide with legal/public name, URL, logo, verified contact and
  `sameAs`.
- The most accurate subtype of `LocalBusiness` only if eligibility and real-world
  business data are verified. Address/geo/opening hours are omitted if unknown.
  `areaServed` describes truthful coverage and does not prove a physical location.
- `WebSite`/`WebPage`, `BreadcrumbList`, and `Article` where applicable.
- `Service` on service pages with provider and area only when verified.
- `Product` only on actual product detail pages; `Offer`, price, availability,
  aggregate ratings, and reviews only when visible and current.
- Do not mark up navigation-only service lists as products or fabricate review,
  FAQ, address, or price properties.

Validate syntax with Schema.org tooling and eligibility with Google Rich Results
Test. Passing validation does not guarantee rich-result display.

## Local SEO and Google Business Profile readiness

- Establish one approved name/address/phone record and keep website, profile, and
  legitimate citations consistent.
- If a service-area business without customer-facing premises, configure the profile
  accordingly and hide the address where required; do not use virtual offices.
- Select the most accurate primary/secondary categories based on actual offerings.
- Add truthful services, description, hours, appointment/quote URL with UTM tags,
  photos, and updates.
- Create a review request process that is voluntary and platform-compliant; never
  gate, buy, seed, or fabricate reviews. Respond without exposing customer data.
- Track calls/forms only with consent-compliant tooling and preserve canonical NAP.

## Search Console and measurement readiness

- Prefer DNS-domain verification; keep ownership with the client and add least-
  privilege users.
- Submit sitemap, inspect priority URLs, monitor Pages, Enhancements, Core Web
  Vitals, security/manual actions, and migration errors.
- Analytics event plan: CTA click, phone/WhatsApp/email click, form start,
  validation error, quote success, support intent, product enquiry—without sending
  names, emails, phones, free text, or other personal data.
- Consent mode/tooling is selected only after legal and platform review.
- Monthly quality review: qualified leads by landing page, organic visibility,
  index coverage, conversions, CWV field data, broken links, content freshness,
  and GBP actions. Rankings are diagnostic, not a guaranteed KPI.
