# Service and Industry Catalog

- **Source:** Client-supplied expansion received 2026-10-13
- **Status:** Approved for information architecture and content planning; operational
  details and claims still require evidence before publication.

## Positioning

Recommended positioning:

> Integrated ICT solutions for homes, businesses, and institutions—from
> connectivity and infrastructure to software, cloud, security, support, and
> digital growth.

Use “full-service ICT provider” only when the visible service catalog accurately
reflects current delivery capacity. Avoid “every technology service,” “everything
IT,” or similar unlimited claims. Present one coordinated provider and connected
solutions without suggesting that every capability is delivered directly, stocked,
licensed, or available everywhere.

## Catalog governance

- The service index exposes clear families first; it must not present more than 100
  equal-weight links.
- Each capability is a child record or structured section of its family. Give it a
  separate indexable page only when search intent, approved expertise, unique copy,
  evidence, and conversion value justify one.
- Capabilities can be marked `available`, `consultation required`, `partner
  delivered`, or `not currently offered`. Only `available` and accurately described
  partner-delivered services may be published.
- Brand names describe compatible products only. They do not imply authorization,
  partnership, certification, or guaranteed inventory.
- Regulated, high-risk, licensed, or intrusive work requires documented competence,
  authorization, scope, and customer permission.

- Schools and universities

- Government institutions
- NGOs
- Hotels and restaurants
- Retail businesses
- Manufacturing companies
- Financial institutions
- Churches and religious organizations
- Construction companies
- Real-estate developers
- SMEs
- Homes and residential estates

Create an indexable industry page only after Edgepoint can provide distinct
customer needs, applicable services, compliance/context, delivery process, FAQs,
and preferably approved evidence for that market. Until then, show the audiences as
non-claiming pathways on the Industries index, such as “Solutions for schools,” not
“Trusted by schools.”

High-sensitivity sectors need extra review:

- healthcare: health/patient-data handling and clinical-system boundaries;
- finance/SACCOs: financial-data, audit, security, and regulatory requirements;
- government: procurement eligibility, accessibility, data hosting, and evidence;
- education/minors: child/student data and safeguarding;
- biometrics/surveillance: lawful purpose, notice, retention, access, and security.

## Navigation and merchandising

Group a Services mega menu into six understandable columns:

1. **Connectivity:** Internet, Wi-Fi, Starlink
2. **Networks:** Networking, fiber/cabling, equipment
3. **Security:** CCTV/access systems, cybersecurity
4. **Digital systems:** Software, websites, automation
5. **Cloud and communications:** Cloud/hosting/email, telecommunications
6. **Advisory and growth:** Consulting/support, supplies, digital marketing

The services index should support “By need” pathways such as Connect a premises,
Build or automate a system, Secure operations, Equip a team, Move to cloud, and
Get ongoing support. These pathways help customers combine services without creating
duplicate SEO pages.

## Quote-routing requirements

The quote form should use dependent, accessible choices:

- service family;
- capability;
- customer type (individual, business, institution, government/other);
- project location and remote/onsite need;
- new installation, upgrade, relocation, supply, troubleshooting, or maintenance;
- timeframe and optional budget band;
- free-text requirements and optional attachment only after the upload risk decision.

Selections route internally to the appropriate team without exposing recipient
addresses. Product enquiries capture category, brand/model when known, quantity,
installation need, and delivery location. Security-testing requests require a manual
authorization step before any technical activity.
